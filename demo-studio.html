Abstract · OriginalL13
\begin{abstract} In the rapidly evolving landscape of large language models, long-context reasoning plays a pivotal role in modern artificial intelligence. In this paper, we delve into the intricate tapestry of attention bottlenecks and propose Sparse Spectral Routing, a paradigm shift that seamlessly integrates frequency-domain token pruning with hardware-aware KV-CSR caching. Furthermore, it is important to note that our framework underscores the importance of dynamic spectral gating under QAT deployment. We define Key-Value Compressed Spectral Routing (KV-CSR) via a projected routing operator $\mathcal{R}_{\theta}(X)$: \begin{equation}\label{eq:routing} \mathcal{R}_{\theta}(X) = \operatorname{TopK}_{k}\!\left(\sigma\!\left(\frac{Q K^{\top}}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) \odot \Phi_{\omega}(X)\right), \end{equation} where $\Phi_{\omega}(X) = \|\mathcal{F}(X)_{[\omega_{\min}:\omega_{\max}]}\|_2$ measures band-limited spectral energy. Crucially, it is worth noting that residual connections \citep{he2018resnet_wrongyear} stabilize gradient flow across deep blocks.
